Most people first hear the words "stamp duty" a few days before their registry, from a broker who quotes a round figure. That figure is often close, sometimes badly wrong, and almost never explained. It is worth understanding how the number is actually built, because it is usually the second largest cheque you will write in the transaction.

## The rate on a sale deed

In Uttar Pradesh, stamp duty on a sale deed is charged at **seven per cent** of the value of the property. On top of that, a **registration fee of one per cent** of the same value is payable, subject to the ceiling notified by the state.

So a property valued at forty lakh rupees attracts roughly two lakh eighty thousand rupees of stamp duty, plus the registration fee, plus small charges for the copying and processing work at the office.

## The part that catches people out: which value?

The seven per cent is not applied to the price you agreed with the seller. It is applied to **the higher of two figures**:

1. the consideration actually being paid, that is, the price written in the deed; and
2. the value arrived at by applying the **circle rate** to the property.

Circle rate is the minimum value the government fixes, locality by locality, for the purpose of calculating duty. If the market in your colony has softened and you have negotiated a good price, the circle rate can easily be higher than what you are paying. Duty is then charged on the circle rate figure, and you pay duty on money that never changed hands.

This works in one direction only. If you pay more than the circle rate, duty is charged on what you actually paid.

### A worked example

Take a 100 square metre plot in a Ghaziabad colony where the circle rate for residential land is, say, thirty thousand rupees per square metre.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Circle rate value | 100 × 30,000 = 30,00,000 |
| Price agreed with the seller | 27,00,000 |
| Value taken for duty | 30,00,000 (the higher figure) |
| Stamp duty at 7% | 2,10,000 |
| Registration fee at 1% | 30,000 |

The buyer pays duty on thirty lakh even though twenty-seven lakh is leaving their account. Had they negotiated the price up to thirty-two lakh, duty would have been calculated on thirty-two lakh instead.

## The rebate for women buyers

Where the buyer is a woman, Uttar Pradesh allows a **one per cent rebate**, but only on transaction value up to **ten lakh rupees**. In practice this caps the benefit at ten thousand rupees.

It is a real saving and worth claiming, but it is not the sweeping concession it is sometimes described as. On a forty lakh purchase it reduces the duty by ten thousand rupees, not by four lakh. Where a property is bought jointly by a husband and wife, the position depends on how the shares are written in the deed, which is a drafting question worth settling before the document is printed.

## Other instruments, other rates

Stamp duty is not a single rate. It depends on what the document does.

| Document | How duty is generally charged |
| --- | --- |
| Sale deed | 7% of the higher of circle rate value and consideration |
| Gift deed to a specified blood relation | A concessional flat amount notified by the state, instead of the percentage rate |
| Gift deed to anyone else | Same as a sale deed |
| Will | Nominal; a will attracts no stamp duty, only the registration fee if registered |
| Lease up to one year | Charged on the rent and term |
| Lease exceeding one year | Higher slab, and registration becomes compulsory |
| Power of attorney | Depends on whether it authorises sale, and on the relationship between the parties |
| Affidavit | Small fixed value, depending on the purpose |

The gift deed concession is the one most often assumed rather than checked. Who counts as a "specified relation" is defined narrowly. A transfer to a son or daughter is usually inside it; a transfer to a nephew or a daughter-in-law may not be. Confirm the relationship against the current notification before budgeting on the concessional figure.

## How the duty is actually paid

Physical stamp paper has largely been replaced by **e-stamp certificates**. The certificate is generated against the details of the transaction, carries a unique identification number, and is verifiable online. In Uttar Pradesh the registration process runs through the **IGRSUP portal**, where the deed details are entered, the duty and fee are computed, payment is made and the appointment at the Sub-Registrar office is booked.

Two practical points:

- The e-stamp is generated in the name of the person liable to pay, normally the buyer. Get the name and the details right at generation, because correcting them afterwards is a separate and tedious exercise.
- Keep the payment receipts. They are needed at the office, and later if any question of deficit duty arises.

## Understating the value is not the saving it appears to be

The suggestion comes up in almost every transaction: write a lower figure in the deed and pay less duty. It does not work the way people imagine, for three reasons.

First, duty cannot fall below the circle rate value in any case, so the "saving" only exists in the gap between the circle rate and the true price.

Second, under **section 47-A of the Indian Stamp Act**, the Sub-Registrar can refer an undervalued instrument to the Collector, who determines the market value and recovers the deficit duty with penalty and interest. These references are not rare.

Third, and most often overlooked, the buyer is the one who suffers. Your purchase price is recorded as the lower figure. When you sell, your capital gain is computed from that lower recorded cost, and the tax on the difference will usually exceed whatever duty was saved. The cash paid outside the deed is also unexplained money in the hands of both sides.

## Budget for the whole registry, not just the duty

When people are caught short on the day, it is usually because they budgeted for the seven per cent alone. The full outgo on a registry normally includes:

- stamp duty on the applicable value;
- registration fee, subject to the notified ceiling;
- processing and copying charges at the office;
- the advocate's fee for drafting and appearance;
- mutation charges afterwards, so the revenue or authority record is updated in your name.

Mutation is the step most commonly skipped. Registration transfers ownership, but until mutation is applied for, the record still shows the previous owner, and that becomes a problem the day you try to sell or raise a loan.

## Before you go to the office

- Get the circle rate for the exact locality and category of property, not for the broad area.
- Have the duty computed on both bases, circle rate and consideration, and see which governs.
- Confirm whether any concession applies to you, in writing rather than on someone's word.
- Generate the e-stamp in the correct name and check the spelling against the identity documents.
- Carry the receipts, the identity proofs of both sides and of two witnesses, and the photographs.
